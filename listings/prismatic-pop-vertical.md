## Description

Bring bold color to your vertical stream with Prismatic Pop: a pop art overlay pack for TikTok LIVE layouts and OBS scenes. Flowing ribbons, orbiting accents and gently turning sparkles frame your camera and gameplay, or one tall camera window, with open space below for readable live comments.

Choose from three looks, each with four layouts. Animated and still options are included, along with a simpler version of every combination.

✦ THREE LOOKS
- Electric Candy: coral red, electric blue, bright yellow and aqua, with flexing ribbons and highlights traveling along their curves.
- Night Signal: deep violet, hot pink, lime and cyan, with bright dots circling elliptical tracks.
- Sugar Voltage: lavender, peach, pink and aqua, with rounded shapes and gently bending ribbon curls.

✦ FOUR LAYOUTS PER LOOK
- Camera and gameplay: two matching 16:9 windows, the camera above the gameplay, with a gap between the two frames.
- Camera and gameplay, compact: the same two windows closer together, the two frames joined.
- Single window, pill: one tall window with fully rounded ends.
- Single window, rounded: the same tall window with softly rounded corners.

In the camera-and-gameplay layouts, your camera is as wide and as tall as your gameplay. The single-window layouts give you one large window for your camera or another single source. That window is tall, so on TikTok LIVE the app's comments can also appear over its lower part. All windows except the pill-shaped one share the same rounded corners, and below the windows the artwork settles into an even tone for comments, without a separate chat box.

✦ WHAT YOU GET
- 12 decorated overlays: 3 looks × 4 layouts.
- 12 matching plain versions, keeping the colors and window frames without decorative artwork or movement.
- Each version in .webm, .mp4 and .png.
- 4 PNG placement guides, one for each layout.
- 76 media files in total, supplied in one ZIP.
- Portrait 9:16 canvas, 1080 × 1920 pixels.
- Decorated video versions: seamless 8-second loops at 60 fps. Plain video versions remain still.

✦ CHOOSE YOUR FORMAT
- WebM: transparent windows, ready to layer over your sources.
- MP4: green-screen windows for software with a chroma-key filter.
- PNG: a still overlay with transparent windows.

The animation follows the decorative shapes. Your window positions and the readable chat background stay steady throughout the loop.

✦ HOW TO USE
- Download the files and unzip the pack.
- Set up a 1080 × 1920 portrait scene in OBS Studio.
- Add the .webm as a Media Source, enable Loop and place it above your other sources.
- Fit your camera and gameplay behind the two windows, or one source behind the single window. Use the matching placement guide as a reference.
- For the .mp4 option, apply a green chroma-key filter to the overlay. For a still setup, add the .png as an Image Source.

Use with OBS Studio or other streaming software that supports the chosen format and transparency or chroma key. This pack provides the artwork and space for comments; your live chat comes from your streaming setup. A chat widget and automatic chat connection are not included.

Made for colorful gaming streams, VTuber setups, just-chatting streams and vertical camera-and-gameplay scenes.
