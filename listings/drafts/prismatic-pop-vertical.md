## Description

Bring bold color to your vertical stream with Prismatic Pop: a pop art overlay pack for TikTok LIVE layouts and OBS scenes. Graphic ribbons, drifting halftone dots and softly breathing sparkles frame your camera and gameplay, with open space below for readable live comments.

Choose from three looks, each with four layouts. Animated and still options are included, along with a simpler version of every combination.

✦ THREE LOOKS
- Electric Candy: coral red, electric blue, bright yellow and aqua, with angular shapes and colorful ribbons.
- Night Signal: deep violet, hot pink, lime and cyan, with orbit-like lines and bright accents.
- Sugar Voltage: lavender, peach, pink and aqua, with rounded shapes and looping ribbons.

✦ FOUR LAYOUTS PER LOOK
- Arched upper window with regular spacing.
- Arched upper window with closer spacing.
- Two rectangular windows with evenly rounded corners and regular spacing.
- The same rounded rectangular windows with closer spacing.

Every layout has two stacked windows for your camera and gameplay, plus an open lower area for comments. The chat background blends into the artwork without a separate box.

✦ WHAT YOU GET
- 12 decorated overlays: 3 looks × 4 layouts.
- 12 matching plain versions, keeping the colors and window frames without decorative artwork or movement.
- Each version in .webm, .mp4 and .png.
- 4 PNG placement guides, one for each layout.
- 76 media files in total, supplied in one ZIP.
- Portrait 9:16 canvas, 1080 × 1920 pixels.
- Decorated video versions: seamless 8-second loops at 60 fps. Plain video versions remain still.

✦ CHOOSE YOUR FORMAT
- WebM: transparent camera and gameplay windows, ready to layer over your sources.
- MP4: green-screen windows for software with a chroma-key filter.
- PNG: a still overlay with transparent windows.

The animation stays in the small details. Your window positions and the readable chat background stay steady throughout the loop.

✦ HOW TO USE
- Download the files and unzip the pack.
- Set up a 1080 × 1920 portrait scene in OBS Studio.
- Add the .webm as a Media Source, enable Loop and place it above your camera and gameplay sources.
- Fit your camera and gameplay behind the two windows. Use the matching placement guide as a reference.
- For the .mp4 option, apply a green chroma-key filter to the overlay. For a still setup, add the .png as an Image Source.

Use with OBS Studio or other streaming software that supports the chosen format and transparency or chroma key. This pack provides the artwork and space for comments; your live chat comes from your streaming setup. A chat widget and automatic chat connection are not included.

Made for colorful gaming streams, VTuber setups and vertical camera-and-game scenes.
